# Delphi Tool

## Purpose

Systematic expert consensus gathering using the Delphi method—anonymous rounds of questionnaires to identify risks, estimate durations, or forecast outcomes.

**Best For:** Risk identification, three-point estimation, expert panels, reducing bias in forecasting.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Create Study** — Enter study name (e.g., "Risk Identification for Project X")
2. **Add Experts** — List expert participants
3. **Design Round 1 Questionnaire** — Open-ended questions
4. **Collect Responses** — Experts submit answers (anonymously)
5. **Analyse & Create Round 2** — Summarise responses, ask experts to reconsider
6. **Iterate** — Continue rounds until consensus reached (typically 2-3 rounds)

---

## Features

### Delphi Method Explained

**Definition:** Structured communication technique where experts answer questionnaires in multiple rounds, with anonymised summary feedback between rounds.

**Purpose:** Achieve convergence on forecasts/estimates without groupthink.

**Process:**
1. **Round 1:** Experts provide independent estimates/opinions
2. **Facilitator Summarises:** Aggregate responses (median, range)
3. **Round 2:** Experts see summary, revise estimates
4. **Convergence:** Responses narrow towards consensus

**Anonymity:** Prevents dominant personalities skewing results.

---

### Creating Studies

**Study Setup:**
- Study Name
- Objective (e.g., "Identify top 10 project risks")
- Expert Panel (names, roles)

**Question Types:**
- Open-ended (e.g., "List risks you foresee")
- Numeric (e.g., "Estimate task duration: Min, Likely, Max")
- Rating scales (e.g., "Rate likelihood 1-5")

---

### Managing Rounds

**Round 1:**
- Blank questionnaire (no prior responses)
- Experts provide independent input

**Round 2+:**
- Show summary from previous round (median, range, outliers)
- Experts reconsider, submit revised estimates
- Facilitator notes convergence (e.g., range narrowed from 10-50 days to 20-30 days)

**Stopping Criteria:**
- Consensus reached (narrow range)
- Diminishing returns (responses stable across rounds)
- Typically 2-3 rounds sufficient

---

### Results Aggregation

**Metrics:**
- **Median:** Middle value (central tendency)
- **Range:** Min to Max (spread)
- **Interquartile Range:** Middle 50% (excludes extreme outliers)

**Convergence Check:**
- Round 1 Range: 10-50 days (wide)
- Round 2 Range: 18-35 days (narrowing)
- Round 3 Range: 20-30 days (converged)

---

## Integration with PMPlan & Risk Register

**Risk Register:**
- Export identified risks from Delphi
- Import into Risk Register as threats/opportunities

**PMPlan:**
- Pull consensus estimates (e.g., schedule forecasts)
- Document in Risk Summary or Schedule sections

---

## Tips

**Panel Size:** 5-15 experts (too few = biased, too many = coordination overhead)

**Question Design:** Clear, specific (avoid "What are the risks?" → "What vendor-related risks do you foresee?")

**Feedback Quality:** Provide rich summary (not just "average = 25 days"—explain range, outliers, reasoning)

**Anonymity:** Critical—use anonymous submission forms, don't reveal individual responses
